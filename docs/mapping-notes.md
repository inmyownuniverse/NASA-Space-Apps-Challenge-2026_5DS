- **What you hear:** A soft, slowly breathing pad chord. The root note follows the average sea temperature (warm water = higher, cold water = lower); extra notes come from the coldest, warmest and middle parts of the ocean. When the currents change a lot between frames the pad gets brighter. If any water goes above 28 °C, a quiet voice one semitone above the root adds tension.

## Lightning from the ISS (`lightning`)

- **What the picture shows:** TODO(Celene)
- **What we measure per frame:** TODO(Aahana)
- **What you hear:** A deep drum hit for each lightning flash. Flashes in the west sound on the left, flashes in the east on the right; flashes further north sound higher. Each hit has a short noise click on top, like the crack of a strike. Brighter flashes hit harder. Up to 4 hits per moment, brightest first, so big storms stay clear.

## GEOS Aerosols (`aerosols`)

- **What the picture shows:** TODO(Celene)
- **What we measure per frame:** TODO(Aahana)
- **What you hear:** Four instruments on one shared chord, one per aerosol type: dust is a low cello-like drone, smoke a crackle, sea salt an airy high shimmer and sulfate soft bells. Each layer gets louder where that aerosol is stronger and goes silent where it is absent; the whole chord rises slightly when the total amount of aerosol is higher. A very dense layer adds a semitone-up voice for tension.
